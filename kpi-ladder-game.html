Build the KPI Ladder

From what the guest pays to what the owner keeps

A 12-minute team game on the projector: two teams, 16 cards, one ladder. Teams call out, the facilitator moves the cards. No phones needed.

PHASE 1 · ~3 MIN
Team A: top down
Start with what the guest pays, end with what the owner keeps.
PHASE 2 · ~3 MIN
Team B: bottom up
Start with the owner's EBITDA and climb back to the drivers.
PHASE 3 · ~3 MIN
Meet in the middle
Compare both ladders, discuss each amber difference, agree.
REVEAL · ~3 MIN
The correct ladder
Rung by rung with a hotel example, then two Aha moments.
